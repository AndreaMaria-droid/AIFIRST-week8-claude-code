# ReveraMethod Wireframe Outline
## Speed-to-Lead Audit Offering | Advisory Positioning

**Document Purpose:** Structural wireframe for one-page advisory website
**Core Offer:** Paid 45-minute Speed-to-Lead Audit
**Positioning:** Calm, clarity-first, low-hype, advisory, trust-driven
**Primary Goal:** Drive qualified audit bookings

---

## SITE STRUCTURE OVERVIEW

**Single Primary CTA:** Book Your Speed-to-Lead Audit
**Navigation Pattern:** Scroll-based with optional anchor navigation
**Conversion Focus:** Remove hesitation, build trust, demonstrate value

---

## SECTION 1: NAVIGATION BAR

### Purpose
- Provide brand presence
- Enable quick section access
- Maintain persistent booking access

### Required Elements
- Logo/wordmark (left-aligned)
- Minimal anchor links (3-4 max): About, Process, Booking
- Single CTA button: "Book Audit" (right-aligned)

### Design / UX Notes
- Minimal, unobtrusive presence
- Fixed/sticky behavior optional (test without first)
- No promotional language in nav
- Consider removing entirely for maximum focus
- If included: muted colors, thin weight, ample whitespace

---

## SECTION 2: HERO / OPENING STATEMENT

### Purpose
- Immediate clarity on what this is
- Speak directly to the core problem
- Establish advisory tone (not sales pitch)

### Required Elements
- Clear, single-sentence headline (problem or outcome)
- 2-3 sentence subheadline explaining the audit
- Single primary CTA: "Book Your 45-Minute Audit"
- Price displayed upfront (no hiding cost)
- Optional: Single trust indicator (years in practice, client count)

### Design / UX Notes
- Generous whitespace - let it breathe
- Centered layout or asymmetric text-focused
- NO hero image or only subtle, abstract visual
- Avoid: excitement, urgency, hype language
- Typography: calm, readable, professional
- CTA should be clear but not aggressive
- Price positioning: matter-of-fact, not promotional

---

## SECTION 3: WHO THIS IS FOR

### Purpose
- Qualification and self-selection
- Help prospects identify if they're a fit
- Build relevance and resonance

### Required Elements
- Section header: clear label
- 5-6 business types listed explicitly (clinics, spas, coaches, etc.)
- 3-4 situation indicators (e.g., "You get leads but too many go cold")
- Optional: single exclusion statement ("Not for e-commerce or retail")

### Design / UX Notes
- Simple list format or 2-column grid
- Use actual business category names, not generic terms
- Avoid: checkmarks, icons, visual clutter
- Consider using visitor's own language
- Whitespace between items for scannability

---

## SECTION 4: THE PROBLEM (UNSTATED)

### Purpose
- Articulate what they're experiencing
- Build recognition without dramatization
- Demonstrate understanding

### Required Elements
- 3-5 common symptoms/situations
- Presented as observations, not pain points
- Grounded in operational reality
- Focus on the gap, not the drama

### Design / UX Notes
- Calm presentation - not "pain point agitation"
- Could be formatted as:
  - Simple paragraph
  - Short list
  - Side-by-side text blocks
- Neutral tone, advisory voice
- No statistics unless essential and credible
- Avoid: fear-mongering, urgency, exaggeration

---

## SECTION 5: WHAT THE AUDIT INCLUDES

### Purpose
- Demystify the offering
- Set clear expectations
- Demonstrate value and process

### Required Elements
- Explicit time commitment (45 minutes)
- 3-5 specific components of the audit
- What they'll receive afterward (report, recommendations, etc.)
- Format/delivery method (video call, phone, in-person)
- What they need to prepare (if anything)

### Design / UX Notes
- Clear, numbered list or process cards
- Specificity over vagueness
- Use precise language ("We'll review X, map Y, identify Z")
- Consider timeline graphic if helpful
- No mystique - complete transparency

---

## SECTION 6: HOW IT WORKS (BOOKING TO DELIVERY)

### Purpose
- Reduce friction and uncertainty
- Show the path from booking to outcome
- Answer "what happens next?"

### Required Elements
- 3-4 step process:
  1. Book session + brief intake form
  2. Scheduled call (45 min)
  3. Receive analysis/report
  4. Optional follow-up or next step
- Clear timeline expectations
- Who they'll work with (you, your team, specific person)

### Design / UX Notes
- Linear, simple flow
- Avoid: overly designed process graphics
- Consider simple numbered list
- Include realistic timeframes
- Transparency about post-audit options (if any)

---

## SECTION 7: WHY THIS MATTERS

### Purpose
- Connect the audit to business outcomes
- Shift from service description to value
- Build urgency without hype

### Required Elements
- 2-3 outcome statements
- Connection to revenue, time, or competitive advantage
- Grounded in reality (not theoretical)
- Optional: brief case example (not testimonial)

### Design / UX Notes
- Avoid: statistics, big numbers, percentage increases
- Use: "You'll know whether..." or "You'll see where..."
- Calm confidence, not excitement
- Could be single paragraph or short list
- No social proof pressure here

---

## SECTION 8: ABOUT / CREDENTIALS

### Purpose
- Establish authority and trust
- Answer "why should I trust you?"
- Humanize the advisor

### Required Elements
- Brief background (2-3 sentences)
- Relevant experience or specialization
- Optional: photo (professional, approachable)
- Credentials or past work (if applicable)
- Why you focus on this specific problem

### Design / UX Notes
- First-person or third-person (choose one)
- Humble expertise, not self-promotion
- Focus on relevance, not resume
- Could be text-only or text + photo
- Avoid: ego, superlatives, "guru" language

---

## SECTION 9: SOCIAL PROOF (OPTIONAL)

### Purpose
- Reinforce trust through others' experience
- Provide pattern validation
- Reduce perceived risk

### Required Elements (if included)
- 2-3 client quotes (specific, not generic)
- Name, business type, location (with permission)
- Focus on clarity/insight gained, not ROI claims
- Consider: logos of past clients if recognizable

### Design / UX Notes
- Optional section - can be omitted entirely
- If included: minimal, understated
- Avoid: elaborate testimonial carousels
- No photos of clients (unless specifically provided)
- Short quotes only (1-2 sentences max)
- Alternative: "I've worked with [business types] in [locations]"

---

## SECTION 10: PRICING & COMMITMENT

### Purpose
- Complete transparency
- Remove price-related hesitation
- Set clear expectations

### Required Elements
- Audit price (exact amount)
- What's included for that price
- Payment timing (upfront, after, etc.)
- Refund or satisfaction policy (if any)
- Optional: what happens after the audit (upsell path if exists)

### Design / UX Notes
- Direct, clear, no games
- Single price point (no tiers, no options)
- If there's a follow-up service, mention briefly but don't push
- Avoid: comparison tables, "most popular" badges
- Consider: "What this is NOT" to set boundaries

---

## SECTION 11: FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

### Purpose
- Address remaining objections
- Reduce friction to booking
- Demonstrate thoughtfulness

### Required Elements
- 4-6 genuine questions
- Concise, direct answers
- Focus on logistics and expectations
- Include:
  - "How long until I can book?"
  - "What if I'm not ready to implement changes?"
  - "Do you work with [specific type] businesses?"
  - "What happens after the audit?"

### Design / UX Notes
- Simple accordion or static list
- Answer honestly, even if it disqualifies some
- Avoid: sales-y FAQ answers
- Keep answers brief (2-4 sentences)
- No "Yes, we're amazing!" answers

---

## SECTION 12: FINAL BOOKING SECTION

### Purpose
- Final conversion opportunity
- Reinforce value without pressure
- Remove last barriers

### Required Elements
- Clear headline (not hype)
- Brief reminder of what they get
- Primary CTA: "Book Your Audit"
- Secondary option: "Questions? Email [address]"
- Calendar link or booking mechanism
- Optional: availability indicator ("Next available: [date]")

### Design / UX Notes
- Calm, confident invitation
- Avoid: countdown timers, limited spots, urgency tactics
- Consider: actual calendar integration visible
- Allow email contact as alternative
- Neutral background - not high-energy

---

## SECTION 13: FOOTER

### Purpose
- Legal compliance
- Additional contact options
- Close the page professionally

### Required Elements
- Business name and location
- Contact email
- Privacy policy link (if collecting data)
- Optional: LinkedIn or professional profile link
- Copyright notice

### Design / UX Notes
- Minimal, functional
- No extensive footer navigation
- No newsletter signup
- No social media icon garden
- Compact, understated

---

## INTERACTION & BEHAVIOR NOTES

### Scroll Behavior
- Smooth scroll if using anchor links
- No parallax or heavy animation
- Content reveals: subtle or none
- Respect reduced-motion preferences

### CTA Repetition Strategy
- Primary CTA appears 3 times maximum:
  1. Hero section
  2. After "What's Included" section
  3. Final booking section
- Same button text throughout
- Consistent visual treatment

### Mobile Considerations
- Single column, linear flow
- CTA buttons full-width or centered
- Generous tap targets (min 44px)
- Readable type (min 16px body)
- No complex grids or multi-column layouts

### Loading & Performance
- Fast page load (< 2s)
- Minimal JavaScript
- No video backgrounds or heavy media
- Consider: static site generation
- Prioritize: readability over decoration

---

## COPY TONE GUIDELINES (STRUCTURAL)

### Headlines
- Clear over clever
- State what it is, not what it does
- Avoid: questions, hyperbole, buzzwords

### Body Text
- Short sentences and paragraphs
- Active voice
- Specific language
- No jargon or industry buzzwords

### CTA Language
- "Book Your Audit" (not "Get Started" or "Learn More")
- Action-oriented but not pressuring
- Consistent throughout page

---

## ELEMENTS TO EXPLICITLY AVOID

### Visual Elements
- Stock photos of diverse teams
- Generic office imagery
- Icons for every bullet point
- Gradient backgrounds
- Parallax scrolling
- Video backgrounds
- Chat widgets or popups

### Content Elements
- "Limited time offer"
- Countdown timers
- "Only X spots left"
- Income claims or ROI promises
- Before/after comparisons
- "Revolutionary" or "game-changing"
- Multiple CTAs or options
- "Free" anything (it's a paid audit)

### Interaction Patterns
- Exit-intent popups
- Email capture before booking
- Multi-step forms
- Social share prompts
- "Powered by" badges
- Cookie consent banners (unless legally required)

---

## DESIGN PHILOSOPHY SUMMARY

**Prioritize:**
- Clarity and transparency
- Calm, confident tone
- Generous whitespace
- Readable typography
- Direct, honest language
- Single path to conversion

**Avoid:**
- Hype and excitement
- Urgency and scarcity
- Visual complexity
- Multiple options
- Sales pressure
- Generic SaaS patterns

**Measure Success By:**
- Qualified bookings (not traffic)
- Completion rate of booking flow
- Quality of intake responses
- Reduction in pre-booking questions

---

## TECHNICAL IMPLEMENTATION PRIORITIES

1. **Speed:** Fast page load, minimal dependencies
2. **Clarity:** Typography and spacing over decoration
3. **Accessibility:** Semantic HTML, keyboard nav, screen readers
4. **Booking Integration:** Direct calendar or form integration
5. **Mobile-First:** Design for mobile, enhance for desktop
6. **Analytics:** Track scroll depth, CTA clicks, booking starts

---

## FUTURE-PROOFING (2026+)

**Avoid:**
- Trendy design patterns
- Platform-specific language
- Dated stock imagery
- Technology buzzwords
- Artificial urgency tactics

**Embrace:**
- Timeless design principles
- Clear value communication
- Human, advisory voice
- Sustainable business model
- Long-term relationship building
