Common Questions

What is the Speed-to-Lead Audit?

A 45-minute diagnostic session where we analyze your current intake and response process. You receive a written analysis showing exactly where you're losing inquiries and how to fix it. $350, standalone service.

What systems does this work with?

We connect to most common platforms including Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, and Zoho. If you use something else, we can work with APIs or webhooks.

How does qualification work?

You define what makes a good inquiry for your business. The system scores each one based on your criteria and routes it to the right person or team.

How long does setup take?

After the audit, most businesses are running within 24-48 hours. Setup includes connecting your tools, configuring qualification rules, and training your team.

Can I start with just the audit?

Yes. The audit is a standalone service. Many businesses use it to diagnose problems before deciding whether to implement automation.

What support is included?

All plans include email support. Professional and Enterprise plans add live chat, phone support, and dedicated assistance.
