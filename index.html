Why this matters

A lead unanswered for 24 hours is a lead evaluating your competitor. Response speed is a measurable advantage, but most businesses can't see their own gaps clearly enough to fix them.

This audit gives you an external perspective on what's actually happening—not what your systems say should be happening. Clarity precedes improvement.

You'll leave the audit knowing exactly which part of your process needs attention, and whether the issue is tooling, workflow, or communication.
